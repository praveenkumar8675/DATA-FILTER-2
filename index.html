About Data Filter

We help businesses turn large, messy datasets into clear answers.

The problem

Businesses collect thousands of sales and customer records, but finding the right ones is slow. Teams lose hours in spreadsheets and miss useful patterns.

Our solution

Data Filter gives teams one fast panel to search, filter and organize records, with results and totals that update instantly.

Our mission

Make data easy to use for every business, whatever its size or location.

Our vision

A world where every team can find the insight in its data without needing a data scientist.

Why choose Data Filter

Practical, honest and easy to adopt.

Fast

Results update as you type, with no page reloads.

Simple

Clear controls any team member can learn in minutes.

Supported

Every package comes with our 3-year service support guarantee.

Global

Runs in the browser, so customers can use it from anywhere.

How Data Filter helps you save time and find opportunities

By narrowing thousands of rows to the few that matter, your team spends less time searching and more time reviewing. Seeing which products and categories perform best can point to opportunities to improve profitability. Data Filter supports better decisions, but we do not promise any specific increase in profit; results depend on how a business uses its data.
